IHC positive percentage calculator

Two counting methods on the same field for comparison: cells counted as squares with dots for positives, and area counting with positive, negative and excluded squares.

Upload a JPG or PNG and set the grid columns so each square covers about one cell. Step 1: with Select cells, click or drag over the squares you want to count. Each selected square is one cell, and the total is the denominator. Step 2: with Positive dot, click each positive cell. Selected squares without a dot count as negative. The image stays in your browser.

Compiled from guideline, trial and assay-label sources. Guidelines and approvals change. Percentages from the grid are estimates, so do not apply these cut-offs to them directly. Confirm against the current guideline and your laboratory's validated method.

Cell method: positive dots ÷ selected squares × 100, where each selected square stands for one cell. Square method: positive squares ÷ (positive + negative squares) × 100, an area estimate. Accuracy depends on matching the grid size to cell size, so check a few squares against the image. This is an aid, not a validated measurement.
