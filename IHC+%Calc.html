Reference thresholds by tumour
Marker What is scored Threshold / categories Notes
Breast
ER % invasive tumour nuclei, nuclear staining Negative <1%; low positive 1–10%; positive >10% (1–100% is interpreted as ER positive) ASCO/CAP 2020. Report % and average intensity; low-positive cases need the recommended comment. Test DCIS for ER too.
PR % invasive tumour nuclei Negative <1%; positive ≥1% Mainly prognostic. St Gallen subtyping has used 20%.
HER2 Membrane pattern, intensity, % of cells 0: none, or incomplete faint ≤10%. 1+: incomplete faint >10%. 2+ equivocal: weak/moderate complete >10%, or intense complete ≤10% (reflex ISH). 3+: intense complete circumferential >10%. ASCO/CAP 2018, affirmed 2023. HER2-low (1+, or 2+ ISH-negative) is not a formal category, but 0 vs 1+ matters for trastuzumab deruxtecan.
Ki-67 % invasive tumour nuclei, standardized visual scoring No universal cut-off. IKWG: ≤5% low, ≥30% high for prognosis in stage I–II ER+/HER2−; 6–29% indeterminate. ≥20% used by St Gallen 2013 and monarchE. Poor reproducibility. ASCO and NCCN did not recommend routine use in the sources I found. Check the current abemaciclib label.
PD-L1 22C3 (TNBC) CPS: PD-L1+ tumour cells, lymphocytes, macrophages ÷ viable tumour cells × 100 CPS ≥10 positive Metastatic TNBC, pembrolizumab with chemotherapy. KEYNOTE-522 neoadjuvant use was regardless of PD-L1.
PD-L1 SP142 (TNBC) IC: % tumour area occupied by PD-L1+ immune cells IC ≥1% positive Not interchangeable with CPS. Check current approvals.
Lung
PD-L1 22C3 (NSCLC) TPS: % viable tumour cells with partial or complete membrane staining <1% negative; 1–49% expressed; ≥50% high Pembrolizumab: TPS ≥1% eligibility, ≥50% for the high-expression setting. Other assays (28-8, SP263, SP142) use different cut-offs.
ALK (D5F3) Cytoplasmic staining in tumour cells Positive: any strong granular cytoplasmic staining; no minimum % Binary read. Confirm per local algorithm (FISH or NGS).
Stomach, GEJ and oesophagus
HER2 (gastric/GEJ) Membranous staining (basolateral or lateral pattern allowed) 3+ resection: strong staining in ≥10% of cells. 3+ biopsy: cluster of ≥5 cells regardless of %. 2+ equivocal: reflex ISH. Differs from the breast criteria. Hofmann-type scoring.
Claudin 18.2 (43-14A) % viable tumour cells, membranous, by intensity Positive: ≥75% of tumour cells with moderate-to-strong (2+/3+) membranous staining Selects HER2-negative gastric/GEJ adenocarcinoma for zolbetuximab. Needs ≥50 viable tumour cells.
PD-L1 22C3 CPS CPS as above Gastric/GEJ: CPS ≥1, ≥5 or ≥10 depending on drug, line and region. Oesophageal squamous: CPS ≥10. Labels and regional approvals change. Check the current label for the drug in question.
Head and neck, cervix
PD-L1 22C3 CPS CPS as above Head and neck squamous: CPS ≥1 (greater benefit at CPS ≥20). Cervix: CPS ≥1. Check current label and region.
Colorectum, endometrium and other MMR-tested tumours
MMR (MLH1, PMS2, MSH2, MSH6) Nuclear staining in tumour cells vs internal controls Deficient: complete loss of nuclear staining in tumour with intact staining in stroma or lymphocytes. Proficient: nuclear staining retained. No % cut-off. Weak, patchy or subclonal staining should be repeated or confirmed by MSI testing.
HER2 (colorectal) Membranous staining Positive in trial criteria: 3+ in >50% of cells, or 2+ with ISH amplification. Trial-derived criteria. Confirm with your assay guideline.
Gynaecological
p53 (endometrium, ovary) Nuclear staining pattern Abnormal (mutant-type): strong nuclear in ≥80% of tumour cells, complete absence with positive internal control, or cytoplasmic. Otherwise wild-type. Pattern-based, not a simple percent. Interpretation depends on site and context.
Neuroendocrine tumours
Ki-67 (GEP-NET) % tumour nuclei in hotspot, ≥500 cells G1: <3%; G2: 3–20%; G3: >20%. WHO grading uses Ki-67 with mitotic count. Separate NET G3 from NEC on morphology.
Lymphoma
MYC / BCL2 (DLBCL) % tumour cells (MYC nuclear, BCL2 cytoplasmic) Double expressor: MYC ≥40% and BCL2 ≥50%. Distinct from double-hit lymphoma, which needs FISH.

Compiled from guideline, trial and assay-label sources. Guidelines and approvals change. Percentages from the grid are estimates, so do not apply these cut-offs to them directly. Confirm against the current guideline and your laboratory's validated method.

Percent positive = positive ÷ (positive + negative) × 100, for squares and for nuclei. Unmarked squares are not counted. This is an aid, not a validated measurement.
